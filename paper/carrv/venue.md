Queried on 2026-10-17 (JST); exact UTC/JST timestamp and HTTP observations are in [venue/query.json](venue/query.json). The official landing page redirects to [CARRV 2024](https://carrv.github.io/2024/). Searches for later editions found no newer official CFP. Direct official `/2025/`, `/2026/`, and `/2027/` URLs returned 404 at this query; this is an observation, not a claim that no future workshop exists. This draft therefore follows the latest located edition, **2024**, pending a new CFP.

| Item | Official 2024 requirement / observation |
| --- | --- |
| Full-paper deadline | August 11, 2024, 23:59 PST (extended from August 4); historical, not an upcoming deadline. |
| Length | At most 6 pages including figures and tables; references may use additional pages. |
| Template | ACM two-column style, linked to the [ACM proceedings template](https://www.acm.org/publications/proceedings-template). |
| Double blind? | No double-blind instruction found; the submission guidelines explicitly request author names, affiliations, and contact email. Do not assume a newer edition has the same policy. |
| Artifact required? | No artifact submission/evaluation requirement stated on the located CFP. This does not certify an exemption under a later CFP. |

Source: official CFP, “Important Dates” and “Submission Guidelines”; retained HTML: [venue/carrv2024.html](venue/carrv2024.html). The ACM template link returned HTTP 403 through the browsing tool. The draft uses the installed, unmodified ACM `acmart` class v1.81 (2021-12-05), `sigconf` two-column option; it does not substitute IEEE formatting. `nonacm` suppresses unassigned ACM copyright/DOI metadata; no margins, font sizes, or column widths are overridden. A future CFP may require a newer class or different metadata.
