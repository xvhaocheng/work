## Three weakest points and concrete writing remedies

1. **Practical relevance depends on the policy and measurement window.** The broad legacy mode's 14.8%–17.6% B slowdown is materially larger than the mainline-derived strict policy's 2.235%/2.472% against matching M0. ML-FQ and ORACLE equal M0 during the workload. The short test enqueues 128 ranges, below the 255-entry effective FQ capacity, and finishes work before its first timer flush; final FQ drain is 501,475 cycles. **Remedy:** put the mainline and legacy results together in the abstract and principal result table; give A API completion, B DMA completion, joint makespan and final FQ reclamation separate labels. State that the FQ result covers deferred invalidation before a first periodic flush, rather than sustained periodic-flush behavior. Call it a conditional policy model with DMA-IOMMU enabled and translated domains, not a running default RISC-V Linux kernel. Evidence: `iommu-platform/out/dead-translation-20261017/README.md:5–14`; `sources/linux/findings.md:11–15,44–55` under that directory.

2. **The diagnosis locates waiting but does not establish a complete resource-level cause.** The old PR=PW=0 experiment has a 19,512-cycle mode-by-co-run interaction: single-run fine-minus-broad is zero, including each command. Its flush-wait increment is 37,854 cycles, with CQ FETCH/REGISTER adding 38,503 and other flush states subtracting 649. This is command-fetch waiting; it cannot be described as PR/PW device drain. The interaction also includes the co-run warm-prefix state. **Remedy:** show the zero single-run difference, signed mutually exclusive accounting and a FIFO predecessor witness; describe 19,512 as an interaction contrast, not a pure memory-resource causal fraction or recoverable benefit. Separate the new PR/PW test overlay, whose model completion points are DDR RLAST/B, from unchanged upstream command handling. Keep the global IOFENCE effect on an unrelated live neighbor explicitly unresolved. Evidence: the same directory's `diagnosis/results.md:3–24,30–65` and `README.md:7`.

3. **The common contribution needs sharper focus.** High dead occupancy produces no benefit from immediate deletion when victim selection ignores validity. The separate masked-tree trap is local, and V2 changes replacement, empty-entry priority and insertion together. **Remedy:** organize the observations around why scope, residency and complete time require separate measurements. Retain the 0% oracle benefit and unchanged R16 case; avoid claims of a new algorithm, upstream masked-tree flaw, or generic dead-entry harmlessness. Evidence: `diagnosis/results.md:67–80`; the partition ablation and its original-source locators in `notes/plru.md`.
