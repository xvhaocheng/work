Only `drivers/misc/idma.c` and new `include/uapi/linux/idma_multi.h` changed in this commit. ABI1 and P0 ABI2 commands and layouts remain. ABI3 uses CAPS `0x0b`, RUN `0x0c`, caps size64, RUN size752, status offset112, stats offset208. [Layout check](multi-abi-validation.json) records native and RV64 compilation. The proposal header/control-flow files are the earlier review artifacts, not installation patches; `multi-driver.patch` is the committed implementation.

The contract is the nine DS0 conditions in [capability-and-plan.md](capability-and-plan.md): V0, A1..N, selected A1; source/destination pages each V1 and equal A pages1/8 or total6 per direction; COPY4096, measured4096, warmup256. The old `idma_run_window` and `idma_batch_window` remain wrappers over shared many helpers. All device references and all mappings are held under the existing global experiment mutex. The stream/observer/HPM context uses `kzalloc`; every exit frees that context. The existing per-stream retain/quarantine policy controls the DMA buffers independently.

After all A warmup/drain, all batch ports are configured; A ports start before V. V's unchanged observer supplies the exact hardware window. After V completion the driver records each A's active/error/issued registers, then STOPs every started port and drains each lane. These register reads and software start/STOP/drain timestamps are sequential. They establish the checked continuous-batch contract and final complete counts, not simultaneous launch times or exact per-A V-window completed work. End in-flight states are captured when the batch-window function returns, before STOP/drain; success is V0/A1. A zero transfer ID retains/quarantines the affected lane and leaves its drained bit clear even if the existing drain helper returns0. Every failure stops/drains all attempted ports while preserving the first error; retained buffers are never released by cleanup.
