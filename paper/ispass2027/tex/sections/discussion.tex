\section{Discussion and Threats to Validity}
\textbf{What the evidence adds.} The completed measurements progress from a macro-scale contrast to a location and then a local dependency: a zero-miss co-run control bounds claims about misses, segmented observations expose internal waiting, and a named-response intervention links an aggressor's remaining PTW service to a neighboring hit's remaining wait.\claim{M-CONTROLS}\claim{M-PATH}\claim{R-E3} None of these stages licenses a complete additive attribution of the batch slowdown. The primary conclusion concerns the tested controller's service organization, with DS50 exposing sensitivity beyond original wiring.

\textbf{Internal validity.} Miss counters use non-atomic software windows, while path counters and elapsed timers have hardware boundaries. Conflating them can create a false zero-miss or per-request claim.\claim{M-OBSERVER} Passive instrumentation can affect area and routing, and similar throughput in different historical images is not a matched measurement of observer overhead.\claim{V-INSTRUMENT} Necessary fixes and the autonomous supply fixture are explicit parts of the baseline. Cycle-to-time conversion uses the configured clock, while the original records leave independent HPM calibration unverified.\claim{V-HISTORY} Positive reported timing slack applies to the constrained paths and does not establish a complete clock-domain-crossing or external-I/O signoff.\claim{V-TIMING}

\textbf{Construct and statistical validity.} COPY is a controlled traffic generator, not an application, a PCIe endpoint, or an accelerator workload trace. The active workload changes more than a miss count.\claim{V-GENERAL}\claim{M-CONTROLS} Most repeats share a boot, and the pooled DS50 repeats span two boots in a fixed collection order.\claim{M-BASELINE} Finite medians, four local complementary interventions, limited replacement layouts, and three composition pairs support descriptive findings, not broad probabilistic or worst-case guarantees.\claim{R-E3-LIMIT}\claim{R-CAPACITY-LIMIT}\claim{R-PN-LIMIT} The incomplete distribution and workload matrices remain visible instead of being extrapolated from means.

\textbf{External validity and provenance.} A single FPGA integration and controller do not represent every RISC-V IOMMU implementation or a commercial memory system.\claim{V-GENERAL} Historical images differ from the repaired baseline and have incomplete loaded-image and software identity checks; those uncertainties remain attached to their evidence.\claim{V-HISTORY} Simulation uses its documented memory model and excludes CPU/Linux execution.\claim{R-INVALIDATION-LIMIT} Published predecessors use different implementations, traffic, baselines, and normalization. We compare their questions and evidence directly, but do not divide their reported absolute bandwidth by ours.\claim{L-COMPARISON}
