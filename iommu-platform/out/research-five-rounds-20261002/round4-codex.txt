Written before reading DeepSeek Round 4. Three disciplinary perspectives from one model, not three independent reviewers. Evidence: shared-facts; actual frontend/transaction-controller source directly reviewed in R2/R3; R3 proposal; verified L1–L10. No new research direction.

Architecture reviewer — current recommendation: major redesign of specification; novelty conditional, not established.
Why publish if successful: an experimentally demonstrated limitation of count-based translation admission on a real serial IOMMU, with a low-cost measured-cost remedy and matched V/A tradeoff against strong feedback controls. A correct token bucket alone is insufficient.
A1 [research-critical] L8 US20250181518A1 already gives per-device/PASID translation-request tokens, configurable rates and caps. L1/L9/L10 establish periodic bandwidth budgets, hardware QoS and service-rate feedback. D-beta identity attribution and D-gamma nonpreemptive admission are engineering requirements, NOT presumptively novel (packet buckets also admit indivisible packets). Residual novelty is only experimentally useful incremental information in actual controller occupation beyond request/miss/byte counts. Must explicitly compare translation request counts as well as miss and bytes, using a common outer feedback rule. Do not claim this literature search exhaustive or novelty certified.
A2 [research-critical] H-M0 same bytes/different locality only distinguishes byte accounting, not miss/request accounting. Need conditional cost variation beyond request/miss counts under NATURAL 4 KiB workloads and policy intervention + ablation. Artificial 128-cycle delay only validates counters. Strong PI may already suffice. Same miss count with varying observed service cost is necessary evidence for this particular differentiation, not sufficient causal proof of V harm.
A3 [spec blocker] R3 3.2 both includes translated VALID&&!READY and excludes address-output waiting. Freeze a single count: propose A-owned translation-controller REQ_TRANS and AWAIT_RESP occupied cycles (read actual state names), excluding COMPLETE translated-address offer/backpressure. Every observed state partition disjoint, sum checked. COMPLETE time and data/post-completion tail remain reported separately; do not label narrow charge all V interference.
A4 [spec blocker] R3 clamp debt forgives unrecorded cost; hysteresis cannot restore lost accounting. Forced R6 release and R8 silent fixed-gap fallback violate budget semantics. Use a sufficiently wide signed balance with explicit overflow detection; overflow inhibits new jobs, drains already accepted/offered jobs and reports failure. No automatic fallback/retry. Positive replenishment implies eventual eligibility only if existing transactions complete and accounting/clock assumptions hold; no absolute progress/latency guarantee.
A5 [spec blocker] Need fully determinate clock semantics, initial credit, first copy admission, debit/refill on same edge, refill rate used (old rate), next-window update, balance boundaries and STOP/VALID drain. Explain test coverage for no missed/double cycle, negative balance, owner switches, long stalls, first/final window. Current batch COUNT asserts VALID independent of ready and must hold it; cannot newly gate an already offered descriptor. `transf_offset` advances at COUNT handshake, not at completion (R3 mistaken citation). WAIT completion derived from backend response; full AW/W/B/R drain is a proof obligation, not yet signed off.
A6 [confound] Frequency/area instrumentation can change measured behavior. All baselines share instrumentation/generator/interfaces, unregulated same-BIT mode; validate fixed-mode equivalence to old behavior. Data path, PTW memory traffic, physical layout and phase can vary with locality. Report segment counters and crossed phase/load controls; don't assert stall percentages add causally. BARE is not a clean no-translation oracle.
A7 [decision] If actual cost has no reproducible incremental policy value, stop M novelty claim; no automatic pivot to an unrelated method after Round5. N/P remain possible architectural comparisons only if explicitly implemented later; M superiority over N/P cannot be claimed.

OS reviewer — current recommendation: important problem plausible, evaluation not ready.
O1 [spec blocker] Zero V completions can mean severe blocking, not V absence. Use trusted explicit V batch-active/start/completion/STOP lifecycle, not no-progress heuristic; active with zero completion must remain active. Need define inactive action (unregulated A at legal boundary), accounting retained/reset only after epoch drain, policy rearm credit convention on V activation, and measure all startup costs. V changes must not erase debt or create free repeated credit.
O2 [fairness blocker] Bytes, requests, misses and cycles have different dimensions. Same numeric Qmax/Bmin is not fair. Calibrate each unit on training data only to a common offered-copy opportunity scale, then allow equal justified tuning budgets; common feedback/update/actuation interface and sensors. Miss-only zero-cost hits need a finite common copy-release limit/physical one-in-flight behavior; don't sneak extra charge into only one comparator. Strong V-only feedback, newly tuned fixed gap, byte, request and miss cost variants are required. With exactly 4 KiB copies, request/byte baselines may be equivalent; document measured request constancy and collapse duplicates only with evidence.
O3 [metric blocker] Matching V target-attainment fraction within 3 percentage points can hide deep rare slowdowns; summing A bytes over different V durations can reward a worse policy. Primary: V fixed-work completion time/throughput, window shortfall distribution and worst observed stall, and A effective bytes per SAME V hardware duration with carry-in/out counts. Match prespecified V time/shortfall tolerances, compare A rates, show full tradeoff. A totals and after-V tail secondary; never drop failed targets.
O4 [statistics blocker] Windows from one run are not independent samples; cluster by randomized paired run/block and retain boot segment. Train/tune and independent-test workloads/seeds separate. Fix order, tuning count, tolerances, repetitions and failure policy before confirmatory results. R3's 2% gain/3pp/2% LUT/.8/.9/.95 targets are arbitrary drafts, not user requirements. Set engineering minimum meaningful effect and feasible goal grid using calibration only. Test uncertainty overlapping zero is inconclusive, not proof of no difference or a forced negative paper. Tight CI within a declared negligible band is a separate outcome. One 4KiB completion is a large fraction of E=4096: tune window to quantization/noise, report response delay tradeoff.
O5 [scope/deployment] Dynamic locality phases need a fixed preloaded hardware address sequence because active writes are locked. Current hardware cannot already do this. Same jobs/dependencies for all policies; phase may stretch in wall time with slowdown, report work-indexed schedule. Synthetic generator cannot establish real application benefit. Define at least one meaningful changing-cost task model and test held-out patterns plus constant-cost negative control; no fake application labels. One bitstream can host probes/modes only after implementation, simulation, timing/resources acceptance; cannot promise one flash succeeds.
O6 [parallel plan] E2 is bounded, independent evidence: V warm→A finite accesses→A drain→first V probe, with equal wait/no-A and small-footprint controls plus actual entry lifecycle. Owner accounting diagnosis and M may share observer plumbing with E2, but E2 positive is not a prerequisite or proof of M. Develop in parallel, execute separately on one board/reset state. Existing 60 P4M runs completed; don't resurrect pending35.

Security reviewer — current recommendation: reasonable performance experiment under trusted configuration; no security guarantees justified.
S1 [scope blocker] Define A as a controlled competing DMA in a trusted configurable platform, not arbitrary malicious peripheral. Source-bound trusted identity must be preserved; a user-supplied spoofable DID is not protection. Kernel owns policy config; current MMIO/hardware trust boundary and all bypass paths need explicit scope. Do not claim adversarial robustness or tenant isolation until bypass/identity/control access are evaluated.
S2 [safety blocker] Policy must preserve translations/permissions/fault behavior, AXI order and stable offered payload. Overflow/STOP cannot withdraw VALID or hang pending responses. Malformed/faulted requests tested as correctness under trusted platform; not proof of complete security. Never force A release to claim starvation safety while silently breaking budget and V goal.
S3 [claim boundary] No bounded service time downstream → no hard-real-time guarantee or infinite-attacker QoS. A-progress and V protection form a tradeoff; an impossible target must be reported. Test feasibility using Solo/minimum A, not R3 'maximum A service'. Completion-based V activity can be gamed even accidentally and must be fixed as O1.
S4 [novelty] IOTLB-SC, DEVIOUS, DSASSASSIN already cover device timing channels and shared-resource leakage; M does not promise cache/queue isolation or eliminate channels. A performance paper may be acceptable without side-channel defense, but security framing must be removed. Negative-control noise is diagnostic, not a defense.
