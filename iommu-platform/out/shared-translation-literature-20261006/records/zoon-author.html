Junhyeok Park



I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science at Georgia Tech. I received my B.S. and M.S. degrees from Sungkyunkwan University, where I completed my M.S. under the supervision of Prof. Seokin Hong.

My research interests are primarily in Computer Architecture, including the design of efficient heterogeneous computing platforms and emerging accelerator architectures.
