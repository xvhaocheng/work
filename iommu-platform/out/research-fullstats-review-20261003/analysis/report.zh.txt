七、结论与限制
  硬件结论：none
  离线可判定项：complete and independently verified
  - per-case classifications, per-condition counts and run-local marks only
  - no case, condition or repetition is upgraded to a global or hardware claim
  - one case never establishes an E2 positive result for the whole matrix
  - three repetitions per condition are engineering repetition: no p-value, no novelty claim, no full-method decision
  - explicit unknowns are reported where the evidence cannot establish a causal chain
  - failed, missing and surprising no-aggressor cases are kept and flagged, never dropped and never retried
  - cost-fullstats is a fixed, independent 21-case collection segment (the three short cost cases plus Solo/A1g0/A8g0/A16g0/A8g1000/A8g2000 in blocks b01..b03); each case is a separate execution (not assumed statistically independent), and group copies are not board-level repetition
  - every cost-fullstats case requires the fullstats block: a missing block or a sticky error is a failure, and the req/select and IOTLB-miss counters are counts of select events and IOTLB lookup misses, not a complete DDT/PDT/PTW type-weighted baseline and not a method-M benefit
  - sampled and fullstats statistics are compared only as means and ranges of the same (req, miss) group: no population median is inferred from the aggregated sums and counts
