# Codex design: completion-stage reservation with early metadata queues

Two DMA / one executing aligned normal4KiB job perDMA, real iDMA, TC, TL and muxes. Test-only snapshot of banked controller; original production source unchanged. A macro selects snapshot in the joint harness; static gate off must reproduce old PN93487/FAST62. No board/BIT/synthesis or novelty claim.

Capture each real private AR handshake into an8-entry virtual-header queue perDMA. Do not wait for an earlier data response to be consumed. TC may translate these heads, including FAST. Only at per-bank completion AR, BEFORE original locked shared completion mux, allocate a real fullresponse slot or a precisely drainable streaming disposition. Original virtual AR and translated completion AR are associated by bank-local order, ID, length and size. A grant is latched and held until actual completion AR acceptance. Do not withdraw downstream VALID.

Mode4: one shared256-beat slot plus2x3-beat private FIFOs. Exact current virtual/physical W segment may stream only if its physical AW is accepted and older same-DMA responses are consumed. Future reads belonging to current virtual W owner wait for their segment to become current; they cannot take the sole pool slot and strand a foreign current owner. Nonowners may reserve the slot for full response reception. Mode5: ordinary allresponses storage, two slots, one permanently assigned perDMA (safe static baseline, not strongest genericpool). Modes4/5 use identical8-entry metadata queues and unchanged real IOMMU FIFO capacities.

Read-admission guard is disabled only for translation in test snapshot: explicit completion-stage guard now owns actual-read safety. Real fullbuffer certificates remain false at BufferDepth3 and are never faked. Full response beats preserve ID/data/resp/last/user, private replay remains independent of sharedR. Release reserved slot on actual DMA RLAST consumption, not TL/AR/PTW completion. Receive and replay sequence counters are distinct; STREAM can never overtake an unconsumed older response. All metadata, AW/W cursors and per-packet disposition counts must be empty at terminal.

Safety checks cover supportedjob shape, descriptor overlap,8queuebound, exact header association and stability, unexpected/extra R, ID/length/last, storage bound, exact STREAM write readiness, W order and packet sequence. Assumes identical read IDs return in AXI order (fixture ID0), fair downstream W service and normal copies; errors/cancel/atomics/unaligned/4DMA and arbitrary memorybackpressure proof are outside this boundedprototype.

Goal: verify early virtual AR acceptance (meta_early counter), recovered measured FAST, byte correctness and drain; compare P/PN hot/cold with ordinary two slots and previous full-private walk baseline. Any savings are DATA capacity only (2096B or4144B vs8192B), not synthesized area. Completion credits/pool + accurate dependency tracking may be equivalent to this mechanism; independent novelty remains unestablished.
